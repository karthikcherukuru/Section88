# SECTION 88

## Register
brand

## Users
Design-conscious unisex streetwear shoppers discovering graphic T-shirts through fashion, culture and creative storytelling, often on mobile.

## Product Purpose
A distinctive custom Next.js storefront with ten clearly labeled sample styles and a persistent local demonstration bag. Prepare the visual layer for future commerce, without implying that orders, subscriptions or contact messages can currently be submitted.

## Brand Personality
Expressive, independent, precise. The original SECTION88_DESIGN_SPEC.md and supplied screenshots establish the editorial direction; the latest user brief supersedes their Shopify implementation notes.

## Anti-references
Generic marketplace templates, floating feature cards, glassmorphism, arbitrary gradients, excessive rounded corners, fabricated garment or business claims.

## Design Principles
Let typography carry the point of view. Use the grid to make exploration clear. Give mobile its own composition. Keep demonstration data transparent. Keep the visual layer independent of future commerce services.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented controls, keyboard operation, native dialog focus management, reduced motion, readable contrast and minimum 44px touch controls.

## Design decisions
Existing brand identity takes precedence over generic skill defaults. DESIGN_VARIANCE 7, MOTION_INTENSITY 4, VISUAL_DENSITY 3. Native CSS and Tailwind v4 form the visual system; no third-party theme. Follow the requested light paper palette and deliberate dark story section, Space Grotesk/Inter typography, Lucide icons and image placeholders. No image generation or image retrieval.
